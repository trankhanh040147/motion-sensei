# Filming guide (send to the player when data quality is poor)

The analysis uses one 2D camera, so the camera angle decides which measurements are possible.

1. **Side view (best for shooting).** Phone at chest height, 4–6 m away, perpendicular to the shooting line, shooting arm facing the camera. Measures knees, elbow angles, timing, ball arc.
2. **Front view (for elbow alignment and drift).** Phone behind the rim or straight in front of the player, about 5 m away.
3. **Whole body in frame for the entire shot**, including the jump and feet on landing, plus some space above the head for the release.
4. **Fixed camera.** Use a tripod or lean the phone against something. Do not follow the player.
5. **60 fps or higher** (iPhone: Settings → Camera → Record Video → 1080p at 60 fps; slow-motion 120/240 fps works too). 30 fps blurs the release.
6. **Light**: good, even light; avoid strong backlight from windows.
7. **Alone in frame**: other people walking through can be mistaken for the player.
8. **Ball contrast**: an orange ball against a plain background tracks best.
9. 10–20 shots per video is enough; 30 s to 3 min.
